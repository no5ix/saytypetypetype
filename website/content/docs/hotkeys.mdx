---
title: "Hotkeys & input"
description: "Toggle mode: press the global hotkey to start, then the stop key to finish. Use a template’s stop key to translate or polish the transcript."
---

Hold mode: hold the hotkey for 0.5 seconds, speak after the start cue, and release to finish. Press Esc to cancel without output.

You can switch apps and look things up while recording. Focus your destination text field before finishing.
