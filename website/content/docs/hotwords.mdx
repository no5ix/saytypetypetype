---
title: "Hotword lists"
description: "Add names, products and specialist terms in Settings → Hotwords. You can keep several groups and activate one at a time."
---

Separate multiple entries with commas and press Enter. Use hotword|weight to assign a weight from 1–10; the default is 4.

Hotword boosting can be Auto, Disabled or Enabled. Restoration recovers your original capitalization, punctuation and formatting after recognition.
